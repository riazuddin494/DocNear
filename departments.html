Medical Departments

Different departments provide different types of medical care.

Medicine

Provides general medical care for common diseases and health problems.

Cardiology

Focuses on heart and cardiovascular related conditions.

Neurology

Deals with the brain, nerves and nervous system.

Dermatology

Provides treatment for skin, hair and nail problems.

Orthopedics

Deals with bones, joints and muscle related problems.

Pediatrics

Provides medical care for infants, children and teenagers.

Gynecology

Focuses on women's reproductive health and related problems.

ENT

Deals with ear, nose and throat related problems.
